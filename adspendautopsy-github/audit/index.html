How to export the right files from Google Ads
  1. Search Terms report (best for spend leaks): click the Campaigns icon in the left nav, then under Insights and reports open Search terms. Set your date range (last 30–90 days), click the download icon at the top right of the table, and choose .csv.
  2. Keyword report: Campaigns icon → Audiences, keywords and content → Search keywords → download icon → .csv. Match type is included by default; add Search lost IS (budget) from the columns tool if you want the budget-cap check.
  3. Campaign report: Campaigns icon → Campaigns → download icon → .csv. Make sure the Cost and Conversions columns are showing before you download.
  4. Bulksheet (structure): in Google Ads Editor, open the Account menu → Export, choose whole account (or selected campaigns), and export to CSV. In Editor Settings, turn on "Include original columns in CSV/ZIP exports" to keep performance data.

Paths follow the current Google Ads interface. If something's moved, search "download search terms report" in Google Ads Help.

A free Google Ads audit that respects your data

Ad Spend Autopsy is a free, browser-based Google Ads audit tool that turns a raw CSV export into a clear picture of where your budget is being wasted. Instead of a generic checklist, it reads your actual Search Terms, Keyword, Campaign or bulksheet data and surfaces the issues that quietly drain PPC budgets — spend on zero-conversion search terms, over-exposure to broad match, campaigns stuck on Maximize clicks, budget caps throttling your winners, thin conversion volume, and bloated ad groups.

Because everything runs client-side, your account data never touches a server — making it safe to run on real client accounts. Use it as a fast directional self-check before a deeper review, then book a teardown if you want prioritised, business-specific fixes.
